The second release candidate of the Cloud Registry UI, published as the
image `elixircloud/cloud-registry-ui:1.0.0-rc2` and tested against
cloud-registry 1.0.0rc2, which it needs: it follows the registry's camelCase
API and its new paths of users' roles, and doesn't work with cloud-registry
1.0.0rc1. It brings the import and sync of service info in the registration
form, a notification bell, tabs, labels and expired invitations in the access
dialog, and a hardened login. Upgrade the registry (with a fresh database) and
the app together, as the [upgrade
notes](https://cloud-registry-ui.readthedocs.io/en/latest/operator-guide/upgrading/#100-rc2)
describe, and check the login settings before the upgrade; users who are
logged in then log in once more.

## Breaking changes

- Needs cloud-registry 1.0.0rc2: the app reads and sends only the
  registry's camelCase names (e.g. `userId`, `registrationRules`,
  `createdAt`) (#204, #205)
- Roles of users are changed at the registry's new paths
  (`/services/{serviceId}/permissions/users/{userId}`), without a fallback:
  with cloud-registry 0.6.0 or 1.0.0rc1 they would go to the wrong user, so
  the app reports such registries as too old; user IDs and groups that start
  with `/` are refused (#190, #191, #193, #196)
- Login: the `issuer` of each login provider and `LOGIN_REDIRECT_URI` must
  be `https` URLs (`http` only for `localhost` and loopback addresses), and
  logging out also ends the session at providers with an
  `end_session_endpoint` (register the app's `/auth/login` as a post-logout
  redirect URI there) (#155, #223, #239, #243)
- The session's cookies are named `__Host-…`, and the app reads only these
  names: users who are logged in when the app is upgraded log in once more
  (#155, #223, #239, #243)
- The form for new invitations requires a label by default; set
  `NEXT_PUBLIC_INVITATION_LABEL_REQUIRED=false` to keep it optional (#187,
  #189)

## Features

- Import a service's public service info in the registration form, and keep
  the new entry in sync with it (#206, #209)
- Show which services are synced or out of sync, with controls to sync now
  and to turn the sync on or off (#207, #210)
- A notification bell in the top bar, with what needs the user's attention,
  e.g. pending services and service types (#208, #211)
- Split the **Manage access** dialog into the tabs **Current Access** and
  **Invitations**, with a page size set by `NEXT_PUBLIC_ACCESS_PAGE_SIZE`
  (#186, #188)
- Labels of invitations, which say who they are for, and expired
  invitations, with new links, removal and **Remove expired** (#187, #189)
- Let admins list the registry among its own services, from the service
  info page (#201, #203)
- Show the registry API's address on the service info page, with a copy
  button (#200, #202)
- A deadline of 30 seconds for every request to the registry, with messages
  that say whether a change may have taken effect, and how long to wait
  after `429 Too Many Requests` (#213, #217)

## Security

- Token requests use HTTP Basic authentication (`client_secret_basic`)
  unless the provider lists `client_secret_post`, and sessions whose login
  provider can't be told are taken as logged out (#155, #223)

## Fixes

- Avoid the request for approvals without a token on each page load, which
  registries that require login refused (#151, #185)
- Show the registry's environment on the service info page as reported,
  without changing its case (#198, #199)
- Keep invitation labels in their table cell (#192, #195)
- Sync now only if the sync is on, and name the pending services in the
  notifications (#214, #215, #218)
- Offer no sync for the registry's own entry, which the registry never
  syncs, and stop asking for notifications that the registry refuses
  (`403`, e.g. a policy without the rules of 1.0.0rc2) (#240, #244)
- Small fixes from the review of this release: what the app shows when the
  registry refuses labels, encoded slashes at the proxy (#194, #197)
